D10: Public registration accepts privileged role

GitHub issue: https://github.com/MuhammadAbdullah8741/SQE-Submission/issues/13
Severity: Critical
Priority: P1
Status: Fixed and verified
Risk: R01
Category: Authorization

Reproduction / review:
Source review: public registration exposes all roles, and UserService.register persists the client-supplied role. Expected public signup role: STU. No external exploitation was performed.

Evidence:
PublicRegistrationSecurityTest; AuthController exposes STU only; UserService forces STU

Root cause:
Client-selected role was trusted during public signup.

Correction:
Force STU in the service boundary and expose only STU in the public form; provision staff through an authenticated admin flow.

Triage:
Regression verifies a malicious ADM field still creates only a student account.
